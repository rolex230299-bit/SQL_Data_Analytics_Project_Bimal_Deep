# SQL_Data_Analytics_Project
A structured, end-to-end SQL analytics project that moves from foundational Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) to Advanced Analytics, following a clear analytical roadmap. This project demonstrates the ability to explore, question, and extract actionable insights from raw relational data using pure SQL.

## 🎯 Objective
To use SQL as the sole analytical engine to answer real business questions in two stages:
- Understand the data — what exists, how it's structured, and what it's telling us at a glance (EDA).
- Go beyond the surface — trends, growth, contribution, and segmentation to support decision-making (Advanced Analytics).

## 🧩 Part 1: Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
1. Database Exploration	What tables/schemas exist? How are they related?
2. Dimension Exploration	What are the unique categories/values in dimension tables (e.g., countries, categories, product lines)?
3. Data Exploration	What does the data look like at a row level — dates, ranges, nulls, distributions?
4. Measure Exploration (Big Numbers)	What are the key aggregated metrics — total sales, total customers, total orders?
5. Magnitude Analysis	How do measures compare across dimensions (e.g., total sales by country, by category)?
6. Ranking (Top-N / Bottom-N)	Who/what are the top and bottom performers (top 5 products, bottom 5 customers)?

## Advanced Analytics
7. Change-Over-Time Trends	How do sales/orders trend monthly, quarterly, or yearly?
8. Cumulative Analysis	What is the running/cumulative total over time?
9. Performance Analysis	How does current performance compare to previous periods or targets (YoY, MoM growth)?
10. Part-to-Whole (Proportional) Analysis	What % does each category/segment contribute to the total?
11. Data Segmentation	How can customers/products be grouped (e.g., by spend tier, recency, frequency)?
12. Reporting	Consolidated SQL views/reports summarizing key business metrics for stakeholders.

## 💡 Key Insights
- 🏆 Top-performing category/product and its contribution to total revenue
- 📉 Notable trend or seasonality identified in change-over-time analysis
- 🎯 Customer/product segments identified and their business relevance
- 📊 Cumulative growth pattern over the analyzed period
